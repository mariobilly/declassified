# Sample photos and clip

The tests use these files. They are free to use.

- `hanneman-portrait.jpg`: Portrait of a Woman, Adriaen Hanneman, ca. 1653. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open
  Access (CC0). https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436630
- `velazquez-portrait.jpg`: Portrait of a Man, Possibly a Self-Portrait, Velázquez, ca. 1635. The Metropolitan Museum
  of Art, Open Access (CC0). https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437874
- `antonello-portrait.jpg`: Portrait of a Young Man, Antonello da Messina, ca. 1470. The Metropolitan Museum of Art,
  Open Access (CC0). https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435581
- `dancer.mp4`: "A Woman Dancing Gracefully" by **Pavel Danilyuk**, from Pexels
  (https://www.pexels.com/video/a-woman-dancing-gracefully-6939968/), free to use under the Pexels licence
  (https://www.pexels.com/license/). Cut to its first 10 s and scaled to 720×1280; it has no sound. The tests cut
  shorter clips from it and add a generated 440 Hz tone to some of them.
